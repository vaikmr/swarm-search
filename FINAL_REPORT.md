# On-chain strategy search — early closeout

**Closed at the user's request on 2026-10-29. Research only; no live deployment.**

## Decision and result

Stop the search and preserve the work. **No strategy qualified for a deployment recommendation.** All twelve entries retain their independent **NOT READY / unverified** status; they are not all economically disproven or rejected.

This is an early closeout, not a claim that the original validation study was completed. No candidate established qualifying T2 historical fork/state replay or T3 elapsed forward paper evidence. The preferred T1 standard of complete, capital-adjusted, fourteen-day realized actor PnL also remained unmet. A partial actor ledger or successful historical view is not a substitute for those standards.

The hypothetical $50,000 portfolio's $2,000–$5,000 monthly stretch goal implies **4–10% net per month** by arithmetic, not observed performance. The work did not establish an accessible new-entrant edge capable of supporting that goal within the charter's loss, concentration and other limits.

## Why continuing the broad search was not justified

The work improved data access, accounting and evidence integrity, but did not establish an executable economic edge. The unresolved work is materially larger than another APY screen or more arithmetic tests: complete cross-chain claims/funding/cost reconciliation, verified historical implementations, realistic entry and competition, genuine stateful strategy replay, and genuinely elapsed forward testing.

There is no supported risk-adjusted ranking, compliant net lending benchmark, achievable monthly income band, or 99%-confidence monthly drawdown certificate. Small-sleeve loss arithmetic and annual component cost budgets do not establish the separate annual tail-probability gate. Reserve custody and shared settlement/issuer exposures were not waived.

These gaps justify **not deploying and not extending this search now**. They do not prove that profitable on-chain strategies never exist.
