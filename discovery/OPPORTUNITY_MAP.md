## Three useful research directions

These are ordered by the value of the next small test, not predicted profitability. They do not authorize a second research wave.

1. **Existing-claim inventory arbitrage using a genuinely new restoration rail.** Circle's 23 October EURC Gateway/Solana expansion and the 2 September USDT0/Stellar deployment are concrete dated leads, not a generic call to trade launches. First verify the same deliverable claim and accessible pools, then compare synchronized, size-specific prices and complete restoration costs against the older rail. EURC Gateway reuse requires an already established balance; it is not unsecured instant finance. Lower transfer cost can also eliminate spreads faster. Literature and economics describe overlapping versions of this mechanism: count it once, not as independent alpha.

2. **Perp-hedged bridge trading, once a real sample exists.** This remains a direct test of the supplied transit-risk hypothesis. The cheapest missing input is the original actor/day/transaction manifest—not another generic simulator. Existing current Hyperliquid metadata/books prove current data access, not the unknown sample's historical hedge feasibility. If the original sample cannot be supplied or recovered, a later study needs a newly prespecified genuine sample rather than substituting the unrelated loan case.

3. **LP-funded rebalancing with an independently cheaper return path.** This has an identifiable payer: the pool benefiting from restored inventory balance. It requires recurring external imbalance and a closing rail that does not undo the payout or silently change the claim. The [current 1,000-unit Ethereum/Arbitrum probe](probes/cbridge-2026-10-29.md) showed no such surplus in either direction and revealed bridged/native USDC mismatch. That tested snapshot does not qualify; other corridors remain untested, not profitable by assumption.

Two other observations should not be promoted into standalone income strategies: tBTC stake-dependent fee offsets have eligibility, capital cost and a small stated cap; contracted redemption advances have provider admission/terms that are not established for a $50k entrant. [Instrument package](workstreams/instruments/findings.md), [settlement package](workstreams/settlement/findings.md).

## Why a perp short does not lock every bridge profit

For matched units and quantity, the accounting includes:

```
spot destination exit − spot source entry
+ perp bid entry − perp ask exit
+ signed funding − all remaining costs
```

A short can offset common market-price movement. If the destination's local premium disappears while the common market price is unchanged, the short does not pay for the lost premium. Wrapper impairment, funding, spread, margin calls, failed bridging and asynchronous fills remain. Adding a hedge also changes winners, not just losers.

Borrowing the receiving-chain token and immediately selling it is a different financing structure. Matching in-flight inventory against token-denominated debt already offsets part of that asset exposure; adding a further short can overhedge. Both debt collateral and perp margin count against total committed capital. [Analytical identity and limits](workstreams/onchain/findings.md).
