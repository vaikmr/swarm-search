## Question and result

The economics package proposes [ECO-IMBALANCE-03](../workstreams/economics/mechanisms.json): receive a legitimate pool-rebalancing surplus, then restore inventory through an independently supported cheaper rail. This needs both a surplus and a complete, exact-asset closing route.

At **09:04:34–09:04:59 UTC**, the public configuration and two estimates provided a useful negative snapshot:

- Both estimates had `bridge_rate: 1`, zero `op_fee_rebate`, and received fewer token units than sent. Neither showed the hypothesized imbalance surplus.
- The cBridge pool symbol `USDC` mapped to **Ethereum native USDC** and **Arbitrum bridged USDC.e**, not Arbitrum native USDC. The latter appeared separately as `USDC_RFQ` with transfers disabled in this configuration. These labels and flags concern this provider's configuration, not every possible route.
- Consequently, assuming a native-USDC CCTP transfer alone restores the quoted bridged-USDC.e inventory would change the asset claim. A separate supported conversion and its costs would have to be verified.

This snapshot does **not** reject other corridors, sizes, times, independent spot discrepancies or a properly specified longer conversion cycle. It supplies no recurrence, capacity, competition or filled-profit evidence.
