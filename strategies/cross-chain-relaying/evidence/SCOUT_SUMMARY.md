# Execution strategy discovery handoff

Date: 2026-10-29 UTC. Scope: Stage 0/1 only, eight execution strategy folders.
This scout did not compute or override the separate lending benchmark.

## Outcome

No candidate has a qualifying T1, T2 or T3 return from this package, and none is
recommended for capital deployment. Missing evidence is not proof of
unprofitability. Return distributions, monthly drawdown confidence and tail
probabilities are not estimated.

The strongest immediate **data-acquisition paths**, not investment rankings, are
Across's attributable fill/refund ledger and UniswapX's actual executor economics:
bounded public API samples worked and selected receipts were independently checked.
Keeper and lawful OFA searcher identities remain weaker leads. Public liquidation
and cross-chain actor addresses exist, but their capital-adjusted net economics
are unresolved. Ethereum L1 builder-dominated arb has no demonstrated eligible
entrant niche here.

## Concrete findings

- CoW standalone/reduced self-funded bonding routes exceed the capital constraints.
  The DAO-sponsored route remains distinct and requires admission, not an assumed
  inaccessible dollar bond. Current BNB-first onboarding needs explicit
  eligibility treatment; no admission or special permission is presumed.
- Across nomination/configuration does not guarantee order allocation. The older
  config's 46 entries are not 46 active firms. The newer registration directory's
  sole example file is not an observed operator.
- Fifty recent Across deposit records contain 46 filled rows, of which 10 have no
  relayer attribution. The 36 attributed rows have six distinct address values.
  This short, biased sample is neither active competitor share nor realized PnL.
- Twenty Base UniswapX filled orders were available despite another observation
  finding no open orders. Empty cached snapshots cannot prove absent activity.
- An official Base receipt confirms a filler-contract address and sender EOA are
  different roles in the same successful execution. They must not be counted as
  independent competing firms.
- A published cross-chain transaction pair was independently found successful
  on Base and Arbitrum with the same sender in December 2023. This validates a
  historical actor pointer, not present profitability or full arbitrage economics.
- 1inch's August 2026 resolver terms document power-delegation and compliance
  requirements. No fixed dollar bond, accepted access or available delegation
  was measured.

Counts and timestamps above are public discovery observations, not performance
tiers. Mechanism/bond figures in the hypotheses are cited documentation facts.
All return and actor-capital fields in the snapshots remain null.

## Validation and remaining access gaps

The public GET collector has four fixed endpoints, bounded payload sizes and
explicit signature/calldata removal. Fixed public receipt probes only permit
receipt and block reads. Tests verify selection, schema rejection, example/zero
address handling, sample-only counts and write-method rejection.

Concrete provider failures are preserved in strategy source notes: guessed CoW
latest-competition routes returned 404; Base Blockscout and a Base publicnode
route returned 403. Official Base/Optimism/Arbitrum receipt access worked.
No paid services, private keys, signing, deployment or live trade were used.

Next meaningful work is a complete actor inventory/refund/reward ledger and
competition-aware historical replay, followed by genuinely elapsed forward
paper observation and independent grading. Recent successful receipts alone
cannot substitute for any of these.
